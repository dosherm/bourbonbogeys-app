Support

Help with the B&B Golf app

Contact
Email dans.home@live.com. We usually answer within a day, and right away during a trip.

Getting in

Open the app, type the trip code your organizer sent you, tap Join Tournament, then tap your name. The app remembers you after that.

Picked the wrong name?

On the player list, press and hold your name and choose Release, then tap the right name.

Fixing a score

Tap the player's card and tap the right number. For an earlier hole, tap ‹ or open Full Card. Once a group's card is locked, ask the organizer to unlock it.

Notifications

Choose which alerts you get in More › Notifications. On silent or in a Focus mode, sounds don't play.

Android

There's nothing to install: open app.bourbonbogeys.com/app in Chrome, tap ⋮ then Add to Home screen.
