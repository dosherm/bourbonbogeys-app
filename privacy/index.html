Privacy Policy

B&B Golf · Last updated November 23, 2026

B&B Golf is a scoring app for a private group of friends who take golf trips together. This page explains what the app stores, why, and who can see it. In short: only what a golf trip needs, seen only by the people on that trip, never sold, never used for ads.

What the app stores

  • Golfers' names on each trip, entered by the trip organizer.
  • Golf information: handicap index, the tees each golfer plays, and (for handicap updates) a golfer's GHIN number, entered by the organizer.
  • Scores and games: hole scores, side-game picks (such as closest to the pin or 30/40 ball choices), shank counts, and the results and payouts the app works out from them.
  • A sign-in ID: when you join a trip, the app signs your phone in anonymously so it remembers which golfer you are. No email or password is needed for players.
  • Organizer sign-in: the organizer signs in with an email address and password.
  • Notification tokens: if you allow notifications, a token so the app can send the trip's alerts (birdies, results, the lock screen card). You can turn these off in the app or in iPhone Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No money is taken, held or moved. The app only works out who won what.

Who can see it

Names, scores and results are visible to the people on the same trip, and to the organizer. The live leaderboard page for a trip can be opened by anyone the organizer shares its link with.

Where it is kept

Trip data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), and notifications are sent through Apple and Expo's push service. These providers only process the data to run the app.

Handicaps

When the organizer adds a golfer's GHIN number, the organizer's own tools look up that golfer's handicap index from GHIN so net scores are fair. The app itself does not sign in to GHIN.

Keeping and deleting data

Trip data is kept so golfers can look back at past trips. To have your name, scores or other information removed, ask the trip organizer or use the contact on the support page, and it will be deleted.

Children

B&B Golf is meant for adults on golf trips and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
